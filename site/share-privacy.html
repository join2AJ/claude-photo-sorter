How your face and number are used

Version draft-2026-11

Who is responsible

The person who invited you and owns this photo album (the album owner) decides how it is used and is responsible for your data. The app runs on their computer or their server. Contact them for anything about your data.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the text-message service that sends your code (it sees your number).

Why we're allowed to (legal basis)

Your clear consent, which you give with the tick box before your selfie. Faces used to recognise people are sensitive personal data, so we ask first, and you can withdraw at any time.

What other people see

  • You see only photos you're in.
  • In those photos, another person's face is clear only if they agreed; everyone else, including people who never signed up, is blurred before the photo reaches you.
  • Your face is shown to others in your photos only if you choose "Show my face". You can limit it to certain people or hide it from certain people.

Your rights

  • Change your mind any time in settings: show or blur your face.
  • Delete: "Blur me everywhere and ask to delete my data" blurs you for everyone straight away and asks the album owner to delete your face code, selfie and number.
  • See or correct what's kept about you: ask the album owner.
  • Complain: to the album owner first; in India to the Data Protection Board, in the EU/UK to your data protection authority.

Children

You must be 18 or older, or a parent or guardian must agree for you. Children's faces are never shown to others unless a parent or guardian agrees.

Keeping it safe

Sign-ins use a one-time code and expire after 30 days. Face codes are stored by the album owner, never in the photos you download. Face matching can be wrong (for example with twins or siblings), so new sign-ups are confirmed by the album owner and no one gets access from a face alone.

Changes

If this notice changes, you'll be asked to agree again before your face is used.
